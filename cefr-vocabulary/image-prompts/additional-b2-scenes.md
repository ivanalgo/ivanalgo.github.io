# Additional B2 scene image prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the Travel, Work, Technology, Environment and Health topic packs.

## Shared production prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: wide visual vocabulary learning scene for a desktop website
Primary request: a rich modern editorial illustration about [SCENE]
Scene/backdrop: [TOPIC-SPECIFIC PEOPLE, ACTIONS, SETTING AND OBJECTS]
Style/medium: polished contemporary editorial illustration, semi-realistic people, layered depth, refined shapes and textures, comparable to a premium educational magazine illustration
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape, multiple clearly connected actions across the canvas, diverse adults and meaningful objects, generous label-friendly spaces
Lighting/mood: bright natural daylight, approachable and realistic
Color palette: a light topic-specific combination of blue, teal, coral, green, amber, violet and warm cream
Constraints: no written words, no labels, no logos, no trademarks, no watermark; do not make it a simple icon, flat symbol or infographic; no dark background
```

## Scene set

- Travel: Planning & Booking; On the Move; Exploring Places; Accommodation & Services; Culture & Responsible Tourism.
- Work: Teams & Projects; Conditions & Performance; Careers & Growth; Communication & Meetings; Leadership & Challenges.
- Technology: Systems & Software; Data & Security; Digital Innovation; Online Communication & Information; Technology in Society.
- Environment: Climate & Energy; Ecosystems & Biodiversity; Pollution & Waste; Sustainable Everyday Choices; Environmental Action & Policy.
- Health: Symptoms & Diagnosis; Treatment & Recovery; Long-term Health & Wellbeing; Prevention & Healthy Habits; Healthcare Services & Support.

The scene-specific prompt for each asset named the relevant people, actions and objects—for example airport transfers and baggage, collaborative project planning, privacy and backups, habitat restoration, or medical diagnosis—while keeping the shared visual constraints above.
