# Music scene image prompts

All five assets were generated with the built-in image generator on 2026-08-24, then exported to WebP at quality 82. The full-resolution PNG originals are retained beside the delivery files. Labels are deliberately excluded from the raster and rendered as interactive HTML.

Shared direction: wide 3:2 adult educational editorial illustration; connected mini-scenes; paper texture; muted teal, ochre and parchment; clear space for overlays; no words, logos, captions, watermark, childish flashcard styling or celebrity likeness.

- `live-performance`: venue entrance, stage, conductor, band, audience response, applause and encore.
- `music-emotion`: headphones and memory, moving concert response, soothing listening, uplifting choir and human connection.
- `creating-performing`: composing, arranging, improvising, rehearsal, interpretation and instrumental technique.
- `describing-evaluating`: close listening, waveform/rhythm, subtle change, contrasting sound worlds and balanced review.
- `culture-society`: heritage transmission, underground scene, cross-cultural exchange, mainstream stage and commercial production.

Use the scene-specific description above together with the shared direction to regenerate an asset. Keep all typography in HTML.
