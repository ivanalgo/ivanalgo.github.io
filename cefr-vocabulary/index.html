About the method

Aligned, not copied

Topic organisation is aligned with Cambridge SMART Vocabulary where practical. CEFR labels use Cambridge or English Profile evidence where available.

Definitions, translations, examples and learning scenes are original. An inferred label is an editorial B2 judgement, not a Cambridge claim.

CEFR belongs to a particular meaning or phrase, not automatically to every meaning of a headword.
